# Krovi UI Direction

## Product language

Krovi is a mobile music product, not a dashboard. Every visible element must have a reason to exist.

## Rosé

Warm editorial direction:
- paper and ink foundations
- dusty rose, berry, burgundy
- strong type hierarchy
- album art as visual material
- restrained highlights
- tactile controls
- subtle print-like grain

## Verdant

Heritage direction:
- ivory and parchment
- olive, moss, forest green
- muted earth brown
- aged brass/gold
- stone-like surfaces
- natural grain and depth

Verdant is intentionally richer than a simple green/gold palette.

## Shared rules

No glassmorphism by default. No random floating cards. No fake metrics. No decorative controls without a product function. No blue tap highlight. No separate desktop information architecture.

## Motion

Motion communicates spatial change, state, hierarchy, or feedback. Major transitions may be expressive; routine interaction should remain fast.

Reduced-motion mode must remain usable.
